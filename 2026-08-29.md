## Problem / what changed (train vs test CE)

**Problem.** Whole-slice holdout made train and test different difficulty. After switching to a fraction split, linear still showed a ~0.02 `train_ce`–`test_ce` gap at 50 epochs (5k params, 4.5M rows). That looked like a broken split. It was not: epoch 0 already matched (1.102 vs 1.102). The logged gap was two **frozen 3 200-row draws with different seeds** (train seed **42**, test seed **0**). Same `best.pt`: seed42 train=0.778 vs seed0 test=0.802; seeds swapped → 0.796 vs 0.790. At **50k** rows, train≈0.795–0.798 and test≈0.792–0.794.

**What we changed**

1. **Split** — dropped `--test-slice`. `--test-fraction` (default 0.10) is a **random** 10% of **each** slice (`--split-seed`).
2. **SGD and CE** — `UniformRowBatchSampler` (row-weighted, not slice-uniform). `train_ce` is frozen row-weighted CE, same measure as `test_ce`.
3. **Metric noise** — `--train-val-subset-seed` defaults to `--test-subset-seed`. End of run prints **matched 50k-row** train vs test CE on `best.pt`.
4. **Scripts** — (1)+(2) were already on linear, medium, DualHidden. (3) was linear-only; now also `train_medium_wdl.py` and `train_nnue.py`. `train_ffnn.py` still last-10% / slice-uniform.
